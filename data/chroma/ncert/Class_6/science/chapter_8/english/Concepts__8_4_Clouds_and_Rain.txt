8.4 Clouds and Rain
How do clouds form? When the sun shines on the water in oceans, rivers and lakes, some of it evaporates and becomes water vapour. This water vapour rises high into the sky. As it goes higher, the air becomes cooler. At sufficient heights, the water vapour condenses to form tiny drops of water. These tiny drops remain floating in the air and appear to us as clouds. Many such tiny drops come together to form larger drops of water. These drops become so heavy that they begin to fall as rain
