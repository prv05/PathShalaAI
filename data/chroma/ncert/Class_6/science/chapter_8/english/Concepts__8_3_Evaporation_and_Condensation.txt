 8.3 Evaporation and Condensation
8.3.1 Evaporation
The process of conversion of water into its vapour is called evaporation. Evaporation takes place all the time, wherever water is present. Have you noticed that wet clothes dry when they are hung in the sun? The water in the clothes evaporates and goes into the air as water vapour. 

8.3.2 Condensation
The process of conversion of water vapour into its liquid form is called condensation. You might have observed tiny drops of water on the outer surface of a glass containing ice-cold water. These drops are formed because the water vapour in the air comes in contact with the cold surface of the glass and condenses.
