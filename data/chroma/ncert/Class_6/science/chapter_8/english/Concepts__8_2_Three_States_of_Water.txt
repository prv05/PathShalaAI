8.2 Three States of Water
Water is found in three states in nature: 


Solid state: Ice, snow and hail are the solid forms of water. 


Liquid state: Water in rivers, lakes, oceans and even the water we drink is in the liquid state. 


Gaseous state: Water vapour present in the air is the gaseous form of water. 

8.2.1 Interconvertibility of States of Water
One of the most remarkable properties of water is that it can change from one state to another. These changes are usually brought about by heating or cooling. 


Melting: When ice is heated, it changes into liquid water. 


Evaporation: When liquid water is heated, it changes into water vapour. 


Condensation: When water vapour is cooled, it changes back into liquid water. 


Freezing: When liquid water is cooled, it changes into ice.
