12.4 The Solar System
The Sun and the celestial bodies which revolve around it form the Solar System. It consists of a large number of bodies such as planets, comets, asteroids and meteors. The gravitational attraction between the Sun and these objects keeps them revolving around it.

12.4.1 The Sun
The Sun is the nearest star from us. It is continuously emitting huge amounts of heat and light. The Sun is the main source of energy for all life on Earth.

12.4.2 The Planets
The planets look like stars, but they do not have light of their own. They merely reflect the sunlight that falls on them. A planet has a definite path in which it revolves around the Sun. This path is called an orbit.

There are eight planets in our solar system. In order of their distance from the Sun, they are: Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune.
