12.5 The Moon
The Moon is the only natural satellite of the Earth. It revolves around the Earth. Like planets, the moon does not produce its own light. We see the moon because the sunlight falling on it gets reflected towards us.

The various shapes of the bright part of the moon as seen during a month are called phases of the moon. The day on which the whole disc of the moon is visible is known as the full moon day. Thereafter, every night the size of the bright part of the moon appears to become thinner and thinner. On the fifteenth day, the moon is not visible. This day is known as the new moon day.
