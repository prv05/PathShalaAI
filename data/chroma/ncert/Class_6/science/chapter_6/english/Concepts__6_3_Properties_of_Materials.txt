6.3 Properties of Materials
Why is a tumbler not made with a piece of cloth?. We choose a material to make an object depending on its properties and the purpose for which the object is to be used.

6.3.1 Appearance
Materials usually look different from each other. Wood looks very different from iron. Iron looks different from copper or aluminium. At the same time, there may be some similarities between iron, copper, and aluminium that are not there in wood.


Lustre: Some materials have a shine on them, which is called lustre. Metals like iron, copper, aluminium, and gold are examples of materials that have lustre. Some metals often lose their shine and appear dull because of the action of air and moisture on them. We, therefore, notice the lustre only on their freshly cut surface.

6.3.2 Hardness
When you press different materials with your hands, some of them may be hard to compress while others can be easily compressed.


Soft materials: Materials which can be compressed or scratched easily are called soft (e.g., cotton, sponge).


Hard materials: Materials which are difficult to compress are called hard (e.g., iron, stone)
