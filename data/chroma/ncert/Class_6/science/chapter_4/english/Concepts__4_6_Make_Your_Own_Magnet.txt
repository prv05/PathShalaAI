Topic: 4.6 Make Your Own Magnet
You can make your own magnet using a piece of iron and a bar magnet.

Place the iron piece on a table.

Rub one pole of the bar magnet along the length of the iron piece, starting from one end to the other.

Lift the magnet and repeat the process about 30–40 times in the same direction.

The iron piece will now behave like a magnet.
