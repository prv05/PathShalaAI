Activities

Activity 2.1: Plan a nature walk to observe plants, insects, birds, and other animals.


Activity 2.3: Collect pictures of various plants and animals and group them on the basis of common features.



Activity 2.4: Observe the height and nature of stems of different plants.


Activity 2.5: Compare the shape and structure of different leaves.



Activity 2.6: Dig out wild herbs to observe and compare their roots.



Activity 2.8: Compare chickpea and maize seeds to observe cotyledons.
