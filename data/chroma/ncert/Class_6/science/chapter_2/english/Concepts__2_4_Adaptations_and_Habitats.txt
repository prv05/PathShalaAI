Adaptations and Habitats
The special features that enable plants and animals to survive in a particular region are called adaptations. The place where plants and animals live is called their habitat. The habitat of plants and animals provides them food, water, air, shelter and other needs for their survival.


The plants and animals that live on land are said to live in terrestrial habitats. Some examples of terrestrial habitats are forests, deserts, grasslands, and mountains. The plants and animals that live in water are said to live in aquatic habitats. Some examples of aquatic habitats are ponds, lakes, rivers, and oceans. Some animals, such as frogs, can live in water as well as on land. These are called amphibians
