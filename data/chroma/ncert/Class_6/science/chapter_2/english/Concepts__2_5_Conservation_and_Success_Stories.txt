Conservation and Success Stories

Janaki Ammal (1897-1984) was an Indian botanist dedicated to environmental work and helped to document and preserve India's rich plant biodiversity. She played a key role in the 'Save Silent Valley' movement.



Salim Ali (1896-1987) travelled across India to observe diversity in birds. He prepared a list of birds and documented their travel routes and habitats. He recorded the regions with high diversity of birds and took measures to conserve these regions. He is referred to as the 'Birdman of India'.



Sacred Groves are undisturbed patches of forests. They are home to different kinds of plants and animals, including numerous medicinal plants. These are protected by the local community and no one is allowed to harm any animals and cut trees in these groves, or disturb the area.



Project Tiger was initiated in 1973 to protect the declining population of the Bengal Tiger. The 'Cheetah Reintroduction Project' was initiated in 2022 to restore the population of the Cheetah.
