11.3 Coal and Petroleum
Coal and petroleum are exhaustible natural resources that take millions of years to form from the remains of living organisms. These are also known as fossil fuels.

Coal
Coal is a hard, black substance mainly composed of carbon. It is used as a fuel in homes, industries, and to generate electricity in thermal power plants.

Petroleum
Petroleum is a dark, oily liquid found deep underground. It is refined to obtain useful products like petrol, diesel, kerosene, and lubricating oil.
