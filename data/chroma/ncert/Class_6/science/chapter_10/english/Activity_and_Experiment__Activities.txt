Activities
Activity 10.1: Let us observe — Identify various components of your immediate environment and classify them as living or non-living.

Activity 10.2: Let us find out — Collect different types of waste and find out if they are biodegradable or non-biodegradable by burying them in soil for a few weeks.

Activity 10.3: Let us create — Make something useful out of waste materials (Best out of Waste).
