10.3 Human Impact on the Environment
As human population grows, our needs also increase. To meet these needs, we are cutting down forests, building more factories, and using more vehicles. These activities lead to:

Pollution: Harmful substances added to air, water, and soil.

Deforestation: Large-scale cutting of trees, which leads to loss of habitat for animals and changes in weather patterns.

Waste Generation: Increase in the amount of garbage, especially non-biodegradable waste like plastic.
