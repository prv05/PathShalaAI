0.4 Waste Management
Waste can be broadly classified into two types:

Biodegradable Waste: Waste that can be broken down naturally by microorganisms, such as fruit peels, paper, and leftover food.

Non-biodegradable Waste: Waste that cannot be broken down naturally and remains in the environment for a long time, such as plastic, glass, and metal cans.

The 3Rs of Waste Management
To reduce the impact of waste on our environment, we should follow the 3Rs:

Reduce: Use fewer resources and generate less waste.

Reuse: Find new ways to use things instead of throwing them away.

Recycle: Process waste materials to create new products.
