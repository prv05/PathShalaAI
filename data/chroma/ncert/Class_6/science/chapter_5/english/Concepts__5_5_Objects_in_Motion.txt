Topic: 5.5 Objects in Motion
Think of some objects you have seen recently. List them in Table 5.4. A school bag, a mosquito, a table, people sitting on chairs or people moving about? The list may also include a butterfly, a dog, a cow, your hands, a small baby, a fish in water, a house, a factory, a stone, a horse, a ball, a bat, a moving train, a sewing machine, a wall clock or the hands of a clock. Which of these are moving? Which are at rest? 

How did you decide whether an object is in motion or at rest? You might have noticed that the bird is not at the same place after some time, while the house is at the same place. On this basis, you may have decided whether an object is at rest or in motion. Motion is a change in the position of an object with time.
