Topic: 3.2 Ingredients of Food and their Sources
Think of a dish, for example, boiled rice. We take raw rice and boil it in water. Just two materials or ingredients are needed to prepare a dish of boiled rice. On the other hand, some food items are made with many ingredients. To prepare vegetable curry, we need different kinds of vegetables, salt, spices, oil and so on.

It may be easy for us to guess the sources of some of the ingredients. Fruits and vegetables, for instance, come from plants. What are the sources of rice or wheat?. You may have seen paddy or wheat fields with rows and rows of plants, which give us these grains. And then, there are food items like milk, eggs and meat, which come from animals.
