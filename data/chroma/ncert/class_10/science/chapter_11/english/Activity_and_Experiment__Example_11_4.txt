Example 11.4

The potential difference between the terminals of an electric heater
is 60 V when it draws a current of 4 A from the source.
What current will the heater draw if the potential difference
is increased to 120 V?

Solution:

Given:
Potential difference, V = 60 V
Current, I = 4 A

According to Ohm’s law,
V = IR

So,
R = V / I
R = 60 / 4
R = 15 ohm

When the potential difference is increased to 120 V,
the current is given by:

I = V / R
I = 120 / 15
I = 8 A

Therefore, the current through the heater becomes 8 A.
