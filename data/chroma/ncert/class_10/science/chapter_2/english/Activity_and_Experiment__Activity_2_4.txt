Activity 2.4

Place a few pieces of granulated zinc metal in a test tube.
n Add 2 mL of sodium hydroxide solution and warm the contents
of the test tube.
n Repeat the rest of the steps as in Activity 2.3 and record your
observations.
The reaction that takes place can be written as follows:

2NaOH(aq) + Zn(s) → Na2ZnO2(s) + H2(g)
(Sodium zincate)

You find again that hydrogen is formed in the reaction. 
However, such reactions are not possible with all metals.
